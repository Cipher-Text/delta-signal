# Social Content Capability Matrix

Ratings: **A** ready with current data; **B** ready with small additional work; **C** requires new aggregation/analytics; **D** not recommended with current data.

| Source/domain | Opportunity | Granularity / freshness | History / forecast | Quality concerns | Visualization | Attribution / disclaimer | Rating / auto-generation safety |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current weather | Daily conditions, temperature, rain, wind | District; current cron about 15 min, persisted latest | Limited stored current history; no forecast in this specific view | Open-Meteo model/grid point, missing values, weather-code interpretation | Metric tiles, simple icon, small sparkline if history selected | “Source: Open-Meteo”; state observation/model basis and timestamp | A; safe for measured condition wording, not causal claims |
| Daily/hourly forecast | Tomorrow/next-days rain, heat, wind, UV | District; refreshed 12h/2h | Forecast by definition | Forecast changes; probability is not certainty; model/grid resolution | 3–5 day strip, rain probability bars | “Forecast by Open-Meteo, issued/retrieved [time]”; prominent “forecast” label | A/B; safe if forecast labels and retrieval time are mandatory |
| Air quality | PM2.5/PM10 and pollutant snapshot | District; hourly rows refreshed every 2h | Stored hourly rows | No repository AQI conversion; model data, health thresholds require authoritative mapping | Pollutant tiles and threshold band only after rule validation | Open-Meteo air-quality source; units and timestamp | B; raw pollutant card safe, health advice/AQI requires work |
| Station water level | River level/trend signal | Station/river; reading timestamp varies | Historical readings | Station coverage, datum/threshold configuration nullable, stale feeds | Line chart, threshold marker, station card | BWDB/source attribution must be confirmed/configured; display station and reading time | B; safe with level/trend; do not call flood without threshold/official alert |

| Union daily climate | Historical comparison | Union; daily | Yes, daily rows | Missing dates, station/grid model, timezone/date semantics | Line/bar chart, “same period” comparison | Open-Meteo, date window and units | C until comparison service and completeness rules exist |

| National emissions | Annual climate/data fact | Bangladesh national; annual | Strong annual history | Indicator definition/units and revision; not current local air quality | Trend line, single fact, year comparison | World Bank indicator code/name/unit | A for factual historical card; no local attribution |

| Satellite radiation | Solar/radiation data fact | District; daily | Stored daily series | Meaning is shortwave radiation, not UV exposure or heat stress | Daily value/small trend | Open-Meteo satellite archive and units | B; explanatory content only |
| Restoration projects | Milestone/progress story | Project/district; event/metric dates | Project activities and metrics | Self-reported/project-specific metric definitions | Progress card/timeline | Project organization and metric definition | B; safe if metric and reporting date are explicit |

## Capability implications

Ready today means a deterministic structured card can be built from one or a small number of existing records without inventing an aggregation. “Ready” still means human approval is mandatory. The first implementation should not treat the rating as permission to auto-publish.

The practical A/B shortlist is: active environmental alert, current weather snapshot, weather forecast, river/discharge forecast, GBIF species observation, national emissions fact, and a neutral verified citizen report. Historical comparison, AQI health interpretation, and weekly/monthly summaries need explicit aggregation/completeness work.
