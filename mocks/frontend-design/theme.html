Design direction — Civic Nature

A system for serious environmental data workflows with restrained color.

Neutral surfaces let data be the focus. Deep green primary anchors navigation and key actions. Teal/blue reads as data, science, and water. Amber and red are reserved strictly for warnings and emergencies — overuse kills their signal value.

Neutral surfacesFor all dashboard and data panels
Green primaryActive nav, primary actions, verified state
Teal accentMaps, water data, environmental readings
Amber / redWarnings and emergencies only — never decorative
